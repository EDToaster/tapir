READING THE STUDY

Does stacking make it smaller?

The sensor and microcontroller may overlap in plan because they sit at different heights. The model also checks the neighboring keys, a USB plug path and an antenna exclusion zone. The detached pointing-stick driver is a third board; it still needs room and a wire route.

The RP2040-Zero build solders the sensor and driver to opposite faces of a daughterboard. Here the MCU is still the wireless XIAO nRF52840 Plus; the RP2040 pinout and header stack are not carried over. No measurements from a salvaged T440 module are assumed.
