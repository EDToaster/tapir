# Tapir Rev A sourcing and qualification

The machine-readable `bom.csv` covers one complete left/right pair. It separates purchased items, included kit contents, lengths cut from one spool and custom fabricated pieces. **Product status: prototype design; no physical parts were inspected, powered or tested, no supplier was contacted, nothing was ordered. In particular, do not connect a cell for live charging.** Source-page checks were made November 21, 2026, America/Denver (November 22 UTC). Distributor inventory is a page snapshot, not a reservation, real-time delivery guarantee or certification of fit. The assembly/inspection plan is in `../validation/BENCH.md`.

## Battery polarity, charger compatibility and USB-only use

The [Adafruit product](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1570) explicitly says the polarity matches Adafruit boards; it also requires a single-cell CC/CV charger ≤100mA, notes protection and no thermistor, and recommends its Micro Lipo charger. The [supplied PKCELL drawing pp. 2, 4–5](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/1570/1570datasheet.pdf) says LP-401230 3.7V with PCM, 105mAh nominal/95mAh minimum, 4.2V charge, standard 0.2C and quick 1C, 0–45°C charging, and includes a “Reverse the plug JST PH-2P” note. Retail 100mAh/100mA is the conservative limit, not a license to charge at 105mA. Do not confuse the cell's charge specification or metrology tolerances with the backup protection trip.

- **JST native pad mapping:** PCB engineering compared the geometric position of [Adafruit Feather M0 Rev C X1](https://github.com/adafruit/Adafruit-Feather-M0-Basic-Proto-PCB/blob/master/Adafruit%20Feather%20M0%20Basic%20rev%20C.brd) to the rotated/native carrier land, including the Eagle/KiCad Y convention and keyed mating face. Eagle pad 2/VBAT maps to native KiCad J1.1, Eagle pad 1/GND to J1.2; [JST's front-view pin 1 is the left contact](https://www.jst.co.uk/downloads/series/TSSC017.01(PH).pdf#page=3). This is corroboration, **not certification for the delivered battery**. With all power removed, trace actual carrier pin 1 through switch and pin 2 to GND. Separately measure the voltage/sign on the disconnected keyed pack with insulated probes or a purpose-built test adapter; map to the mating orientation. Do not touch two terminals with one probe, use a continuity meter across a live cell, or use wire color as proof. Record board/pack lot and photo of keyed mating faces. Do not connect if uncertain.
- **Charge current:** The [Seeed Plus schematic package](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-BLE/Seeed_Studio_XIAO_nRF52840_Plus.zip) v1.1 U2 value is **BQ25100**, even though the footprint name contains BQ25101. R18 2.7k from ISET to GND; R12 2.7k from ISET to MCU **P0.13/HICHG**. The [TI specification §6.5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25100.pdf#page=5) gives KISET=129/135/145 AΩ in this range. Default high impedance, no pulls: nominal 50mA; if resistance tolerance is ±1% (assumption, not a released Seeed tolerance), about 47.3–54.25mA. Pulling P0.13 low parallels the resistors: nominal 100mA, worst 108.5mA, over the retail limit. **Do not enable fast charge.** Do not confuse right-hand shield D13 (pointer rail GPIO) with MCU port P0.13 (charger control); record its state through reset, stock bootloader, DFU, firmware and sleep. R20=10k on charger TS; pack has no cell thermistor. Ambient guidance does not substitute for measuring actual pouch temperature.
- **Voltage mismatch, mandatory stop:** The [TI BQ25100/101 output regulation table, p. 5](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25100.pdf#page=5) explicitly specifies 4.16/4.20/4.23V at VIN=5.0V, IOUT=0–250mA and TJ=0–125°C with nominal TS state. The [Adafruit-linked pack spec pp. 2, 4](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/1570/1570datasheet.pdf) specifies 4.2V CV and says not to exceed its charging voltage; no normal-charge tolerance reaching 4.23V was found. A [second PKCELL family datasheet](https://www.batterypkcell.com/uploads/LP401230-105mAh-3.7V.pdf) gives 4.2V cutoff and 4.325±0.025V **protection detection**, not a CV allowance; it is not evidence of identical harness revision. A passing sample or a BMS interrupt cannot establish compatibility across tolerance. Obtain written supplier allowance for voltage, current, termination and exact assembly/revision, or change the design under control and verify it independently. Until then test USB without a cell; characterize the charger with approved cell simulation only.
- **Favorable but indirect evidence:** The [seller's 1570 listing](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1570) specifically links the [Micro Lipo 1304](https://www.adafruit.com/product/1304); [Adafruit's guide](https://cdn-learn.adafruit.com/downloads/pdf/adafruit-microlipo-and-minilipo-battery-chargers.pdf) identifies the 4.2V MCP73831 charger. Its [DC characteristics](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/datasheets/MCP73831.pdf) allow 4.168–4.232V at the stated test conditions. This is useful context when querying the supplier. It is not express approval of a different charger, termination algorithm or temperature range for the exact 1570 lot and does not lift the stop.
- **OFF and charging order:** S1 OFF opens the cell's positive path to XIAO BAT+, so no intended charge path exists even with USB present; **the keyboard may still be powered by USB**. Verify isolation/reverse feed with a current-limited simulator, not a real battery. With the cell physically absent and protected against shorts, USB-only firmware and basic functional tests can proceed subject to independent PCB checks. Do not mistake OFF for a fully de-energized board.

## Protection, ESD and release evidence

- **Exposed ports:** USB-C is factory-mounted **on the XIAO**; the USB D+/D− nets never leave it for the carrier. The [vendor schematic](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-BLE/Seeed_Studio_XIAO_nRF52840_Plus.zip) shows USB data through series resistors to the MCU, not a basis for claiming an external IEC-rated TVS on those lines. Do not add an unreachable carrier TVS or claim product-level immunity. All loose J2 wires and J1 are internal service points. Specify insulating case, protected work area, anti-static handling and no external exposed signal pin. Before consumer release, test powered and unpowered sample discharges at accessible USB shell, pointing/click surfaces, screws and seams with an appropriate lab; a failure requires rerouting/protecting the interface or revising the controller, not a BOM-only fix. There is no discrete ESD SKU stuffed in Rev A.
- **Custom part/assembly dependencies:** Per-hand PCBs, top/bottom cases and separator are not stock items. The cap fit, sensor bridge attachment (including an interposer if needed), printed/tolerance material, 105mm wire storage and head/driver strain relief remain unqualified. Nonconductive optional feet, liner and any added bonding are not performance assumptions. Do not put adhesive on the pouch or strain element or select a conductive/rigid bond by guesswork. Do not describe `bom.csv` alone as a consumer-complete manufacturing release while these gates are open.
- **Test scope:** Source checks, land-pattern inspection and whole-design automated checks establish document/digital evidence only. Test every physical purchased part and every channel of the assembled unit using `../validation/BENCH.md`: incoming identity/dimensions; orientation/continuity; USB-only bring-up and every key/control; shifted power, clock, reset and analog wiring; cable, screw and shell fit; RF, wake/idle/runtime and travel durability. Pass/fail requires a measured unit/lot, conditions, raw readings, operator/date and corrective action for failures. No ESD, thermal, run-time, life, stick-force, drop or lithium-cell physical result is asserted here.
