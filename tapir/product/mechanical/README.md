# Tapir Rev A bilateral enclosure prototype

This directory generates editable, assembled-coordinate case CAD for both **different** keyboard halves. The design is not cleared for ordering, printing, battery charging, or on-body testing. A nominal CAD result does not verify a mold detail, hand clearance, emitter orientation, radio performance, battery safety, or printed strength.

## Inputs, datums and build

The spatial authority is [the common geometry](../design/geometry.json), not a second list of manual chassis placements. Product geometry uses mm, +X right, +Y toward the palm, carrier top Z=0. CAD uses (X,−Y,Z) and each half retains its own origin. Real populated KiCad STEP is imported per half, in XY from the KiCad user-origin export; translate it **−1.595 mm in Z** so top-side component seating is at 0. The bare exported dielectric is not the whole copper/mask stack. Check the PCB export again if the stackup changes. Assembly STEP keeps reservations in a separate named group; hide it and the extra schematic context when inspecting physical surfaces.

From the `tapir/` folder, regenerate the common geometry and both populated ECAD STEP files first; then:

```sh
uv venv --python 3.12.15 .venv-mechanical
uv pip sync --python .venv-mechanical/bin/python product/mechanical/requirements.txt
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv-mechanical/bin/python product/mechanical/preflight.py
OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv-mechanical/bin/python product/mechanical/build_case.py
# For WebGL previews only, without assembly proof or interchange exports:
.venv-mechanical/bin/python product/mechanical/build_case.py --meshes-only
```

The lock includes build123d 0.13.0, OpenCascade 8.0.1.1.0 Python wheel and Shapely 2.1.2. `design.py` implements printed bodies; `harness.py` computes battery-wire lanes; `kernel.py` constructs BReps and checks STL topology; `build_case.py` handles assembly, motion and reports. `--hand left|right` and `--build DIR` enable isolated reruns. The ignored `build/` holds assembled-coordinate `{left,right}/assembly.step`, `printable-parts.step`, named per-part STLs, per-hand and joint `clearances.json`, and `viewer-manifest.json`. Individual STL files are in assembled coordinates, **not bed oriented**. Printables must re-open from STEP with the same labels, single-solid count, bounds and volume; every binary STL edge must have exactly one oppositely wound mate.

The right bridge has 15.84 mm² of supported foot area; its two tabs extend 7.06 mm² beyond the cover opening in plan. The STEP includes four proposed 0.15 mm dielectric support/bond annuli only at the sensor mounting lands, not across the sensing area. These are design allowances, not a validated adhesive or supplier-approved attachment. The [four-conductor analog head/driver mapping](../electronics/pointer-harness.md) is distinct from the five host wires to J2. The canonical proposed head/driver run rises above the bridge, clears its front edge and only then descends through the bezel slot to the unmeasured driver's edge. Its 37.082 mm centerline is not a wire cut length. The five host wires, final strain relief and supported solder joints at the real driver remain a hard first-article gate; they are not silently treated as an FFC or included in the analog motion pass.

To assemble, load the hex nuts from below; temporary removable bench tape can retain them during inversion. Lower the already-soldered carrier straight 24 mm into the tray, no removable modules, caps or external plugs attached. Seat it on the four bosses and install PCB screws. Do not use a screw to force the board into place. With power off, install the separately insulated cell, lower its separator straight down 18 mm with the PH unplugged, and route the unshortened leads above the separator into the correct service opening. Support both ends and the plug housing when making or breaking the connection. Confirm polarity separately before applying power.

On the right, place the pointer bridge with the electronics isolated and the four sensor leads relaxed. The two palmward tabs must lie flat on the carrier. Install the remote driver and route its four head wires plus five host wires clear of neighboring keys. With the MBK caps and rubber nub still removed, lower the bezel straight by 24 mm, ensuring tabs engage and no lead or pouch is caught. Install three exterior case screws, then fit the removable caps and nub. Reverse for service; unplug USB, isolate the battery and unload the cable guide before lifting. The exposed controller access well is intentional; this is not a spill-resistant, lint-proof or fully sealed enclosure.

## USB is tested as two different things

The generic gauge projects 38 outward from each shared mouth, width 12.35 and Z0..6.50. The named-cable check uses [Qualtek 3021091-01M](https://www.qualtekusa.com/images/Cable%20Assemblies/PDF_2/3021091-01M.pdf): 6.65 ±0.20 mm exposed metal, 36 mm reference molded-end dimension, and cable Ø3.5 ±0.2. Because the vendor drawing does not give shoulder width and height, use the [USB-IF Table B-1](https://www.usb.org/sites/default/files/USB%20Type-C_Compliance%20Document_Rev_2_1a_June_2021.pdf) overmold maximum **12.35×6.50**. The external 36 mm molded-body proxy travels 36 mm straight, reserving 72 continuously from the F.Fab face. The 6.85 max tip conservative gauge is 8.50 wide (over the USB-IF 8.28 upper width) ×2.43 high, centered at Z3.25. The straight jacket is bounded by 3.70 square. Model the molded shoulder flush to the nominal F.Fab mouth and verify that the shell and printed structures do not prevent reaching that face. The drawing is linked, not reproduced. **There is no vendor C-to-C plug STEP or toleranced mating stop here:** a real plug, mated engagement, actual datum, internal tongue, latch retention and bent cable still need a sample.

## Validation interpretation and first-article gates

The final recorded run used canonical geometry SHA-256 `20ad221093737c378c4b5ea2b33760189b9a99a57a39fdb5763000540c22b89d`. All **44 left and 57 right** nominal checks passed. The 73 populated ECAD instances on each half were tested against every printed part; these are digital solids and inherited fit models, not physical specimens. Outer dimensions include the selected fasteners, pack, pointer and native populated assemblies, exclude optional feet, external cable and reference sweeps, and are notably larger than the bare carrier bounds.

The delivered mesh manifest SHA-256 is `f96dc3a5ddd4cd9ac6e2a415c783f126b069beb65e702ec5019233223a7789da`; the aggregate clearance report is `9d247eec172b70c90adde82305c8148f6dafcb25d6a205d080490784a33bf45d`. These hashes identify the artifact bytes for this handoff, not a promise that subsequent STEP timestamps re-export identically. A deliberate upward displacement of the bridge by 0.50 mm yields 1.71994 mm³ common volume with the cover tabs, confirming that the anti-lift check detects intended capture rather than silently passing all separations.

The script tests the union of the motion volume, not only end poses: the as-soldered board lowers into its bosses; the capless cover passes switches and the bridge; the organizer lowers above the unconnected cell; all keys traverse their full swept cap envelope; the straight plug reaches the case face. Geometric broad-phase rejection uses exact XY extrusion layers/STEP bounds. Candidate clashes are confirmed with OpenCascade solid common volume; zero permits intentional seating contacts, not a guarantee of tolerance margin. The complete KiCad populated STEP is also tested against every printed part. The case and PCB hardware are smooth analytical fit bodies, not modeled screw threads. Each clearance report includes hashes for its source geometry, native board, exact printable meshes, printable STEP and assembly STEP.

The cable study's XYZ data is `build/{left,right}/battery-wire-105mm.json` (`centerlineXYZ_mm`, canonical +Y palm); display CAD uses −Y. Bounded material checks use conservative 0.25 mm Z bands. The continuous nonlocal self-clearance check considers all segment points separated by ≥6 mm along the same lead; among those closer than 1.20 vertically, the worst XY separation is 4.669 on the left and 4.559 on the right, both exceeding the 2.20 envelope. Adjacent bends within 6 mm, conductor strain, lifetime flex and actual stack pressure are not verified. Run the fast preflight before expensive STEP exports.

For a future, separately authorized first print, the tray's flat underside is the natural starting face. Make the bridge from its deck side with the feet growing upward, then inspect the small pilot bores and deck flatness; do not let a slicer erase the 0.8 deck or small button flexures. The upper bezel includes unsupported hood spans and asymmetric flexures. Inspect the actual process, support removal and surface finish before assuming it prints without supports. The low-force driver pocket laterally bounds its nominal board but does not qualify an adhesive or a retained analog solder joint. Any supplemental strap must act only on a verified mounting land, not on a connector, sensitive sensor region or cell.

Before spending: inspect PCB electrical status, missing component geometry, the battery manufacturer's limits/polarity and charger behavior. Before assembling/using a first article: check the actual print dimensional scatter with a nut/port/bridge coupon, key and paddle bottom-out, pull-out and pointer side force, sensor bonds/drift, untouched harness with safe bends/strain relief, freedom from pouch pressure, seated USB cable, insulation, divider flex and BLE in the final case. Do not treat theoretical coil capacity, an assumed cable bend or a clear static shell as a passed end-to-end wiring, mechanical strength, or radio test.
