# Tapir first-article acceptance card

State: **no physical build, measurement, flash, or fit is claimed here**. A KiCad report, compiled UF2, kinematic screen or simulated event does not complete any row below. A supplier listing is not an incoming inspection. Release only the tested serial numbers and revisions; re-open affected rows after substitutions, board/CAD/keymap changes or print process changes.

## Entry and records

Start with an unpowered first article of each half and a nonconductive dummy pouch. Record: operator/date; left/right serials; PCB revision and copper source hash; firmware hashes; exact module revisions, cell, switch, cap, connector and cable markings; actual printer/material/orientation; instruments, calibration and setup photos. Retain failed trials. Use the component rows and designators from `results.json` as the inspection traveller; give every populated instance a disposition. Enter measurements as measured values with units and uncertainty, not “seems fine.” Strictest applicable vendor limit wins. Suggested acceptance numbers here are project test targets, **not component ratings**.

**Stop rules:** Do not connect a real cell until polarity, insulation, chemistry, regulator/charger limits, and current setting are verified against exact datasheets and the selected assembly. A missing limit is a block, not permission to experiment. First perform unpowered inspection, then use protected equipment and current-limited USB with both cells physically disconnected; use a qualified battery simulator, not a real pack, to characterize the charge circuit. Do not short a cell, force a plug, crush a pouch or lever on a sensor. Isolate any suspect sample. No fabrication or purchase is authorized.

**Rev A charging hold:** The [Seeed Plus v1.1 design archive](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-BLE/Seeed_Studio_XIAO_nRF52840_Plus.zip) labels its controller BQ25100. The [TI BQ2510x electrical characteristics](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq25100.pdf) allow 4.23 V regulation, while the [Adafruit/PKCELL pack specification](https://cdn-shop.adafruit.com/product-files/1570/1570datasheet.pdf) gives 4.20 V CC/CV and does not document permission to charge at 4.23 V. Verify the JST connector polarity by DMM on the actual disconnected pack; the drawing calls for a reversed JST plug. Do not mate or charge a real cell until voltage compatibility is established by the pack maker or the charger is redesigned and qualified. One acceptable measured unit, or the PCM overvoltage trip, does not establish full-tolerance safety. USB-only bring-up on an independently inspected, cell-disconnected board is distinct from enabling cell charging.

| E03 | Right load switch, PS/2 and sensor/driver | Scope VDD, CLK, DATA and RESET from cold boot through ready, idle, power gate, suspend and wake on battery and USB. ## Host event and fault matrix

For M05, measure the actual lead-pair cross-section and neutral lead length from the pack exit to the selected plug datum. Resolve the listing's 105 mm versus the drawing's 100 mm value and obtain the manufacturer's minimum neutral-axis flex radius or other explicit assembly guidance; a modeled curved path is not approval. Assemble an unpowered inert cable surrogate with comparable stiffness, verify that its outgoing and returning paths do not pile up, and use witness strips to test where the cassette/hood could pinch it. Inspect the entry at the protection board and the soldered terminals after at least ten service cycles on the dummy. Do not use the live cell as a dimensional fixture or trim the factory wires.

Use a timestamped raw HID/OS event capture, not just a text editor. Record connection state, source half, matrix position, layer, expected and actual reports for each case. Disconnect or delete only test-device pairings. Keep logs separate for simulation and hardware.

| ID | Test | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|
| H01 | On each implemented layer, walk all 42 keys and 3 controls at least once, press then release; compare physical legends and function to the signed map. Check home-row hold-tap as tap, as hold with opposite-hand key, and with the three extra controls. | All intended positions match, no swapped/mirrored thumbs, no ghosting with tested chords, exactly one make/break, no latched modifiers/layers. Transparent positions produce the correct lower-layer event. |
| H02 | Right pointer moves in all four directions while left performs left click, right click, click-and-hold drag, and scroll-held movement; press/release in both orders. Test diagonal/reverse movement, zero movement, multiple reports, both signed directions and extended holds. | Correct axis/sign; drag remains depressed until the physical button releases; scroll redirects motion only while held, with no spurious cursor travel; release restores normal motion and all HID buttons zero. Test over actual split link, not on a single board. |
| H03 | Hold a click/scroll, interrupt the link, reconnect; later suspend host, power down one half, release during sleep, reconnect by key wake, and repeat in the reverse order. Test right unavailable and left unavailable, cold-boot in either order and with no pointer attached. Repeat 30 times per edge case. | No stuck key, modifier, layer, pointer button or ghost wheel tick after recovery. No input while relevant source is off. Both halves reconnect, normal typing and motion resume. Document whether pointer movement is intentionally not a wake source when its rail is gated. |
| H04 | Run a mixed typing + pointing session on each claimed transport/host with the case closed, then capture a stationary-pointer interval after boot and after warm-up. Repeat charge insertion/removal while tracking. | Suggested use trial: 30 min per host/transport; no unintended disconnects, ghost events, drops, or repeated pointer jumps. Agree on a drift envelope before test; record counts/time/temperature under zero load, not just visual impressions. |

For E04, the Rev A target in `product/requirements.md` is 1 h pointing, 7 h connected and 16 h asleep each day for 7 days. With 100 mAh and assumed 80% usable, each half must average at most 0.47619 mA (11.4286 mAh/day). Record actual battery-terminal charge for those states, plus reconnect/wake bursts. Verify the right driver is gated off in sleep; a driver that only enters its own low-power state is not equivalent to a disconnected rail. Require typing-key wake on both halves, test on both sides of the 15-minute timeout, and include a held drag across that boundary. On charge, repeat startup in ROM/DFU and application modes. If any mode drives P0.13 low, use the simulator until the mode is corrected: its two parallel current-setting branches can exceed 100 mA.

## Sign-off

Attach measured tables, waveforms, calibrated photos, host event traces, raw current logs, exact supply/cell datasheets and firmware hashes. For each ID record PASS_PHYSICAL, FAIL, or BLOCKED; only an identified sample and measured acceptance can earn PASS_PHYSICAL. Stop on shorts, ambiguous cell specification or negative pouch margin. A second reviewer signs the remaining risks and owner fixes; design completion, prototype authorization and eventual production qualification are separate decisions.
