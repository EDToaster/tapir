# Pointer above the right-thumb XIAO: evidence and limits

A small bridge can carry the Sprintek above a controller: [humanplayer2’s physical RP2040-Zero build](https://codeberg.org/humanplayer2/trackpoint_on_rp2040zero) demonstrates that arrangement. It does not demonstrate a fit on the non-Sense XIAO nRF52840 Plus. Use the RP2040 build to choose the assembly concept, the XIAO board files to lay out the new bridge, and a physical coupon to validate height, reach, retention and radio performance.

## What was actually built

- **Seven original images.** In [the original Reddit gallery](https://www.reddit.com/r/ErgoMechKeyboards/comments/1wln421/trackpoint_on_rp2040zero/), images 1 and 2 show the hardware and an uncalibrated side profile, 3 shows the PCB layout, 4 the capped assembly and its accessible USB port, 5 the bare sensor and post, 6 the driver side, and 7 the separate components. The [archived post with original gallery metadata](https://api.pullpush.io/reddit/search/submission/?ids=1wln421) identifies the author and links that same Codeberg repository. These photographs do not establish any Z dimension.
- **The same sensor, not a new donor.** The author names the detached 3.3 V Sprintek SK8707-01-004 in both [the build instructions](https://codeberg.org/humanplayer2/trackpoint_on_rp2040zero) and [the post comments](https://api.pullpush.io/reddit/search/comment/?link_id=1wln421&size=100). The sensor is soldered to the top of a custom adapter and the controller/driver to its underside. Six header positions attach the adapter on the RP2040-Zero’s non-port side; the instructed 5 mm of protruding pin before soldering and trimming is not a finished spacing. The post is not evidence of a directly bonded sensor, interchangeable XIAO shield or alternate pointing-stick SKU.
- **Wired QMK reference, not a BLE schematic.** The author uses GP1/GP2 for PS/2 data/clock, 3.3 V and ground, and two otherwise electrically unused support positions; the adapter has two 4.7 kΩ resistors. The author says the click inputs are not connected to the adapter, uses keyboard keys instead, and discusses a nice!nano possibility rather than a tested port ([build](https://codeberg.org/humanplayer2/trackpoint_on_rp2040zero), [thread comments](https://api.pullpush.io/reddit/search/comment/?link_id=1wln421&size=100)). Tapir still needs its own GPIO budget, switchable rails/pull-ups if used, ZMK config, split pointing and LED/reset access checks.

## XIAO datums for the stack

[Seeed’s non-Sense Plus product page](https://www.seeedstudio.com/Seeed-Studio-XIAO-nRF52840-Plus-p-6359.html) specifies a nominal 21 × 17.8 mm PCB. The following dimensions are derived from the actual Edge.Cuts, F.Fab and footprint placements in the [Seeed Plus v1.1 KiCad archive](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-BLE/Seeed_Studio_XIAO_nRF52840_Plus.zip), linked in the [vendor’s hardware resources](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO_BLE/). They describe PCB artwork, not a tolerance guarantee.

| --- | --- |
| Carrier outline | Board measures 17.780 mm wide × 20.955 mm long. Its USB-end board edge is Y = −10.478 mm. The full assembly extends beyond this edge. |
| USB1 receptacle | The top-side fabrication rectangle is 9.00 mm wide × 7.35 mm long, X = −4.50…+4.50 and Y = −11.99…−4.64 mm. Its front projects ~1.51 mm beyond the board, so overall plan length including that rectangle is ~22.46 mm even before a cable is inserted. |
| ANT1 antenna | The top-side `ANTENNA-AN3216` placement origin is X = −1.80, Y = +9.41 mm, only 1.07 mm inward from the end opposite USB. This is a component origin, not a keep-out boundary. |
| Stack height | The Plus section of [Seeed’s wiki](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO_BLE/) links a shared Sense DXF, not a Plus-specific toleranced assembled side elevation. A [non-Sense Plus distributor listing](https://eu.robotshop.com/fr/products/seeed-studio-xiao-nrf52840-plus) gives 5 mm overall height; do not call 5 mm the USB shell height above the XIAO’s top face, and do not add XIAO PCB thickness to it a second time. Confirm top and bottom features on the sample. |

For reproduction in the Seeed file’s native coordinates: Edge.Cuts extremes are X = 138.0236…158.9786 and Y = 96.1136…113.8936 mm; ANT1 is at (139.095479, 103.205275); USB1 is at (153.1366, 105.0036), rotated 180°. The USB fabrication rectangle spans local X = −7.35…0 and Y = −4.5…+4.5 mm ([Plus PCB archive](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-BLE/Seeed_Studio_XIAO_nRF52840_Plus.zip)). The [published Plus-SMD host footprint](https://files.seeedstudio.com/wiki/XIAO-KiCad-Library/New_XIAO_Series_Footprints.zip) does not supply an RF keep-out rule or a standalone Plus assembly STEP; its model entries are library-path references. The Plus exposes nine extra GPIO pads underneath, not nine pins accessible from above ([Zephyr board reference](https://docs.zephyrproject.org/latest/boards/seeed/xiao_nrf52840_plus/doc/index.html)).

## Tapir-specific checks before approving a stack

- **Separate support from electrical connection.** Carry thumb pressure through an independent carrier-mounted bridge or bosses. Do not load the USB shell, XIAO solder joints, castellations or antenna. If the driver stays in its existing bay, label it there and route its connections; if it moves under the bridge, add its outline, both populated faces and interconnect explicitly. The two-layer adapter in the [RP2040 build](https://codeberg.org/humanplayer2/trackpoint_on_rp2040zero) and the sensor/driver in [Sprintek’s drawing](https://docs.holykeebs.com/datasheets/SK8707-01.pdf#page=6) are not one flat sensor block.
- **Measure Z, not just plan overlap.** Starting at the Tapir carrier, record the mount/solder standoff, XIAO underside and shell top, bridge-to-highest-component clearance, adapter and sensor thickness, bare post, cap and any extension. Include the driver only where it physically goes. Recheck key bottom-out, thumb access, post deflection, cover closure, and whether the USB opening remains at the perimeter. Sweep an actual plug, including its wider molded shoulder, straight in and out; the 9 mm receptacle drawing is not a plug envelope.
- **Give the antenna an exposed edge.** Avoid overlapping the antenna end with metal sensor/driver plates, daughterboard copper, fasteners or pouch foil. Make any no-metal/no-copper zone in the model explicitly provisional; no Plus-specific guaranteed clearance distance was established from [the vendor files](https://wiki.seeedstudio.com/XIAO_BLE/). Compare assembled and unstacked BLE connection stability, RSSI and reconnect/wake with and without a hand resting over the thumb cluster. A positive mechanical clearance is not an RF pass.
- **Budget pointer power separately.** At 3.3 V the [Sprintek table, p. 5](https://docs.holykeebs.com/datasheets/SK8707-01.pdf#page=5) gives typical 2.09 mA active, 1.44 mA idle with Z-Tap on, 0.65 mA idle with Z-Tap off and 0.040 mA in power-down. For the [reference 110 mAh protected pack](https://42keebs.eu/shop/parts/lithium-polymer-battery/), pointer-only continuous 0.65 mA idle would ideally consume nominal capacity in ~169 h (7.1 days); this excludes the MCU, radio, regulation, battery derating and typing. It is not a keyboard runtime prediction. Measure supply and battery current with USB disconnected, including deep sleep, data-line back-power when the pointer rail is off, reset and wake-to-first-motion under ZMK.
